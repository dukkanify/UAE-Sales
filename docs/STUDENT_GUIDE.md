# Student guide — ATPL PASS

## Getting started

1. Open the app URL provided by your school.  
2. Go to **Login** and enter your email.  
3. Enter the one-time code sent to your inbox (demo environments may show a fixed code).  
4. You land on the **Student dashboard**.

## Learning

- **Courses** — open enrolled courses, continue lessons, track progress.  
- **Planner** — set goals and study targets.  
- **Notes** — keep study notes linked to your learning path.  
- **Quizzes** — attempt published assessments; review results when available.  
- **Certificates** — download issued certificates; public verify page is available without login.

## Live classes

- Open **Classes / Calendar** to see upcoming sessions.  
- Join when the instructor starts the session (Zoom link when configured).

## Communication

- Read **announcements**.  
- Join **communities** and message instructors/peers where enabled.  
- Open support conversations from the communication hub.

## Billing

- Browse the **catalog**, place orders, and review order history under billing.

## AI assistant

- Use the AI hub / FAB for study help and recommendations (subject to school feature flags).

## Tips

- Use a modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari).  
- If you are locked out, contact your administrator — do not share OTP codes.
